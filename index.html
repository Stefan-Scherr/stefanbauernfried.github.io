About

Stefan Bauernfried, PhD, is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Genentech. His research focuses on innate immune sensing, inflammasome biology, host-pathogen interactions, viral immunology, and herpesvirus biology. He is particularly interested in mechanisms of inflammasome activation and host defense against viral infection.

Research Interests

  • Innate immunity
  • Inflammasomes
  • Viral immunology
  • Host-pathogen interactions
  • NLRP1
  • NLRP3
  • Herpesvirus biology
